Currently building

Starbie

A tiny motion-controlled digital pet, basically a desktop Tamagotchi. It's built around a XIAO ESP32-C3 with a motion sensor, temperature and humidity readings, an OLED screen, an RGB LED, and Cherry MX switches for input.

Made for Hack Club's HalfLife. Schematic and PCB are designed in KiCad, and the firmware is in progress. See the repo. Evogen is on pause while I work on this.
