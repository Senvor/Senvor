# Incognito mode myth (2026-11-26)

Incognito mode only stops *your browser* from saving history, cookies and form data after you close the window.

- Websites still see your IP address and can track you during the session.
- Your ISP, school or employer network can still see which sites you visit.
- Logging into an account (Google, etc.) ties activity to you again.
- Downloads and bookmarks you make are kept.

Think of it as a notebook that gets erased, not a cloak: the people watching still saw you write.
