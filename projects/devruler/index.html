01What I built

An everyday ruler made for developers, designed from scratch in KiCad and made with JLCPCB. It packs hardware footprints for quick sizing, everyday commands for when your brain blanks, basic SI unit conversions, and Hack Club and Forge branding.

02What I learned

I got hooked on the idea after seeing a PCB ruler in a hardware video, found Hack Club's hardware-only program Forge, and designed the whole board myself.
