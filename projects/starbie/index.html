Starbiedesigned

A motion-controlled desktop pet on a custom PCB.

  • XIAO ESP32-C3
  • Arduino
  • KiCad
  • MPU6050
  • DHT11
  • OLED

What I built

A tiny motion-controlled digital pet, basically a desktop Tamagotchi, on a custom PCB around the XIAO ESP32-C3. It detects movement with an MPU6050, reads temperature and humidity with a DHT11, shows info on an OLED, gives feedback with an RGB LED, and takes input from Cherry MX switches. Schematic and PCB designed in KiCad for Hack Club's HalfLife, following their Week 1 PCB tutorial.

What I learned

My second hardware project, so I pushed for something more interesting than the first one, and HalfLife gave me a reason to actually design, build and document it instead of leaving it as an idea.
